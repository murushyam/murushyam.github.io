01 About

A geoinformatics-trained atmospheric scientist who treats fire weather, carbon pools, and satellite time series as one coupled system.

What I work on

Forest fires across India have grown more frequent and destructive. My thesis, Decoding Atmosphere–Biosphere Flux Nexus in the Phase of Wildfire using AI Techniques over Heterogeneous Forest Ecosystems, follows three contrasting landscapes — moist deciduous Similipal, dry deciduous Bandhavgarh, and montane forests near Chamba — through pre-fire, active-fire, and recovery windows.

I combine microwave and optical remote sensing, flux diagnostics (sensible and latent heat, VPD, soil moisture, AOD), and models that range from WRF-Chem to attention-based deep learning, causal fuzzy time series, and quantum reservoir computing. The through-line is practical: earlier fire-weather warning and a clearer picture of carbon and moisture recovery.

Outside the thesis I have worked on PM2.5 nowcasting with explainable AI, methane during the COVID lockdown, inland water quality with hyperspectral sensors, InSAR subsidence mapping, and quantum algorithms through the Womanium Quantum + AI program.

02 Research

Same fire physics, three ecosystems. Magnitude changes with fuel, elevation, and moisture memory.

Odisha · open

Similipal Biosphere Reserve

Moist deciduous. 2021 fire: SH +22.4%, LH −33%, VPD to 5.04 kPa. ~47% AGB loss.

Madhya Pradesh · open

Bandhavgarh Tiger Reserve

Dry deciduous, lower fuel loads. More restrained flux response; soil-moisture coupling still steep.

Himalaya · open

Chamba & 2022 Himalayan fires

Montane buffer: SH +42.9%, milder LH drop. 2022 UK fires: 16.75% biomass loss, uneven recovery.

Thesis question

How do wildfire-driven shifts in energy, moisture, and carbon pools differ across heterogeneous Indian forests — and can hybrid ML / physics-informed / quantum models forecast fire weather and burnt area at 1–30 day horizons well enough to serve SDG-aligned early warning?

Forecasting stack: PACI-ICPDA, a seven-phase pipeline of classical baselines, variational mode decomposition, causal models, LSTM-attention hybrids, and adaptive XGBoost meta-stacking for fire weather, fire counts, and burnt area.

Land–air couplingSoil moisture is the critical amplifier of surface heating after fire.
Aerosols & air qualityAOD more than tripled over Similipal fire pixels in 2021.
ResilienceUttarakhand 2022: 40.85% post-fire recovery and a net biomass gain after monsoon recharge.
